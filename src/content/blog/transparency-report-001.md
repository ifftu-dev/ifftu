Our first transparency report. Where the money goes, what we're working on, and every decision we made this month — explained.

Radical transparency isn't a buzzword here. It's a commitment. Every month, we publish a full accounting of everything IFFTU does: finances, decisions, progress, and failures. This is the first of those reports.

## WHAT WE DID

January 2025 was our founding month. The majority of our time was spent on organizational infrastructure: establishing legal structure, setting up development environments, and beginning work on Alexandria.

We made 47 commits to the Alexandria repository, established the core protocol specification, and began prototyping the peer discovery mechanism. All code is available on our public GitHub.

## DECISIONS MADE

**Rust for core infrastructure.** We chose Rust for Alexandria's core due to memory safety, performance, and the strength of its ecosystem for systems-level networking code.

**AGPL-3.0 license.** We chose the AGPL to ensure that any modifications to Alexandria must also be open source, even when deployed as a service.

## WHAT'S NEXT

February will focus on the content-addressed storage layer and initial networking primitives. We expect to have a proof-of-concept that can share documents between two peers by month's end.

We'll also be publishing our first blog post explaining IFFTU's mission and the reasoning behind Alexandria.
